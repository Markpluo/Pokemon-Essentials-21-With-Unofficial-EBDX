Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026. Copper & Current is a hobby project run by Mark. This page says what the demo stores and why.

What the game keeps

  • Your save stays in your own browser (local storage). It is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes it.
  • Settings such as sound and animation speed are stored the same way.
  • Invite cookie. When you enter a valid invite code, the site sets one cookie (cc_invite) so you don't have to type the code again. It holds an expiry date and a signature, nothing about you, and lasts 60 days.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking cookies.
  • We don't sell or share anything, because we don't collect anything.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your IP address and basic request details to deliver the pages and protect the site. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Fonts load from Google Fonts (Google's privacy policy).

Online play

The demo is single-player only. If online play is added later (seeing other players, chat, trading), it will need an account, and this page will be updated before that goes live.

Questions

Ask Mark, the person who gave you the invite code. You can ask what is stored and have it removed at any time.
