Copper & Current

A Pokémon fan game set in Copenhagen

The lights of Copenhagen are flickering, the harbour has gone cold, and a new energy company promises power without price. Grab a starter at the Palm House, ride Professor Ørsted's Rotom cargo bike through Nørrebro, Nyhavn and Christianshavn, and find out who is draining the city.

This is a private demo for invited friends. It covers the Prologue and the first six chapters, about nine hours with side quests, and plays in any modern browser, on a phone too.

What's in the demo

  • Five startersSprigatito, Charmander, Popplio, Eevee or Pikachu. Your rivals Lukas and Maja pick theirs after you.
  • Copper and CurrentThe Rotom coil on your bike lets you armour up or supercharge a move once per battle.
  • A real cityDronning Louises Bro at rush hour, the Round Tower, Nyhavn's quay, the Christiania lakes.
  • Day and nightThe clock runs while you explore. Some Pokémon and people only come out after dark.
